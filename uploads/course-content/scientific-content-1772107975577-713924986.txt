First: Causes of the Campaign
1️⃣ Political and Military Causes

France was in conflict with United Kingdom.

Britain relied heavily on the route to India through the Red Sea.

Napoleon aimed to weaken Britain by cutting off its route to India.

France wanted to expand its influence in the East.

2️⃣ Economic Causes

Egypt had a strategic trading location.

Control of Eastern trade routes would strengthen France economically.

3️⃣ Scientific and Cultural Causes

Spreading the principles of the French Revolution (liberty, equality).

Studying Egypt’s ancient civilization.

Napoleon brought more than 150 scientists and scholars.

Second: Major Events of the Campaign
1️⃣ Landing in Alexandria (1798)

The French army arrived in Alexandria.

The city was captured quickly.

2️⃣ Battle of Shubra Khit

Fought between the French army and the Mamluks.

The French won due to superior military organization and weapons.

3️⃣ Battle of the Pyramids (Imbaba)

Took place near the Pyramids of Giza.

Napoleon defeated the Mamluks led by Murad Bey.

Napoleon famously said:
“From the heights of these pyramids, forty centuries look down upon you.”

4️⃣ Battle of the Nile (Aboukir Bay)

The British fleet led by Horatio Nelson destroyed the French navy.

The French army became isolated in Egypt.

5️⃣ Egyptian Revolts
🔥 First Cairo Revolt (1798)

Led by scholars of Al-Azhar.

Suppressed violently by the French.

🔥 Second Cairo Revolt (1800)

More organized than the first.

The French commander Kléber was assassinated.

Third: The End of the Campaign

Napoleon secretly left Egypt in 1799.

Leadership passed to General Kléber, then General Menou.

Britain and the Ottoman Empire intervened.

The French surrendered in 1801.

The campaign officially ended.

Fourth: Results of the Campaign
✅ Political Results

Weakened Ottoman control in Egypt.

Opened the way for Muhammad Ali Pasha to take power.

✅ Scientific Results

Discovery of the Rosetta Stone.

Helped scholars later decode hieroglyphics.

Publication of the famous book Description de l'Égypte.

✅ Social Results

Introduction of the printing press.

Establishment of administrative councils (Diwans).

Exposure of Egyptians to modern European systems.

Overall Evaluation

❌ It was a colonial invasion aiming at control and expansion.

✅ However, it marked the beginning of Egypt’s modern transformation.
